# Customer Master

Customer Master reuses canonical party identity and must not create a conflicting duplicate Customer record system. The canonical table/model is `individuals` / `App\Models\Individual`, filtered server-side to `type = 'customers'`. Employees, vendors, transporters, agents, labourers, and master-party rows remain separate subtypes in the shared table.

The live company has 248 Customer rows. Existing fields include name, company name, GSTIN, PAN, phone, WhatsApp, email, status, and audit metadata. Customer Code did not exist in live data; the new nullable `customer_code` field does not backfill or invent codes and is unique only when supplied for current customers. GSTIN is trimmed/uppercased and format-validated for new changes, while legacy blanks/placeholders remain compatible. PAN remains separate and is validated when supplied. GSTIN is not made globally unique because live duplicate GSTINs exist.

The canonical address source is `individual_address`, linked by `individual_id`, with multiple Billing (`b`) and Shipping (`s`) rows, State relation, default flag, and status. Customer Master provides separate address CRUD. Billing/Shipping addresses supplied with a Customer must be validated server-side as belonging to that Customer. Referenced addresses are logically protected from deletion. Master address changes never rewrite transaction snapshots.

Customers are company-scoped through `company_id`. Active and Inactive customers remain historically readable; active-only customer autocomplete remains available for new selections. Deactivation does not cancel orders, delete addresses, or rewrite history. Referenced customers cannot be hard-deleted. Name/contact/address corrections remain allowed; referenced Customer Code, GSTIN, and PAN changes are blocked to avoid changing legal historical meaning. Sale Orders retain `customer_id`, billing/shipping address IDs, and billing/shipping text snapshots. Customer Master does not redesign Sale Orders, pricing, Work Orders, printing, dispatch, or Lab Tests.

Customer is not a Frontend User and no login or portal account is created. Customer is also distinct from the future Vendor and Transporter masters even though those entities share `individuals`. Customer Master uses dedicated `customers.*` Admin permissions, centralized Audit Log, and a separate permission-aware navigation link from Individuals (All Parties). No CRM, receivables, credit, accounting, or sales pipeline is included.
